### policy
1.Policy is the behavior of the scheduler that decides what runs when.
2.A scheduler's policy determines the OVERALL FEEL of a system and is responsible for optimally utilizing processor time.

### types of processes

### I/O bound processes
1.spends much of its time SUBMITTING and waiting for I/O requests.
2.Submitting : requesting an I/O operation from the operating system.
3.such a process is runnable for only short durations, because it eventually
blocks waiting on more I/O (keyboard, mouse input, network/disk I/O, GUI etc).

### processor bound processes
1.They spend much of the time executing code.
2.They tend to run until they are preempted as they do not regularly block on IO requests very often.
